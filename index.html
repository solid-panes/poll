poll

A pane for the SolidOS data browser that finds a time for a call or a meeting. Offer hours on one or more days; people answer Yes, If need be or No for each, and the best hour so far is marked. Every hour is an exact moment, shown to each person in their own time zone. It is kept in your pod, is one module loaded by link, and uses the format of SolidOS's own scheduling pane.

Try it

The data browser asks before it adds a pane from a site it has not seen. Allow it, and a sample opens. The sample is read-only: to answer, make one of your own with New Poll in a folder's Add menu.

The pane: https://solid-panes.github.io/poll/poll-pane.js

Source and details: github.com/solid-panes/poll
