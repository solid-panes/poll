poll

A pane for the SolidOS data browser that keeps a poll in your pod. Options can be dates and times, or anything else to vote on. People answer Yes, If need be or No, and the leading option is marked. It is one module, loaded by link, and reads and writes the format of SolidOS's own scheduling pane.

Try it

The data browser asks before it adds a pane from a site it has not seen. Allow it, and a sample poll opens. The sample is read-only: to answer, make a poll of your own with New Poll in a folder's Add menu.

The pane: https://solid-panes.github.io/poll/poll-pane.js

Source and details: github.com/solid-panes/poll
